FAQ

Common questions.

Do you issue gaming licences?

No. Gaming licences are granted only by the official regulator of each jurisdiction. We are an independent consultancy: we prepare and manage your application and liaise with the regulator on your behalf. The decision is always the regulator's.

Can you guarantee approval?

No one can guarantee a regulator's decision. What we can do is assess your eligibility up front and make sure the application is complete, consistent and well-evidenced.

How much does a licence cost?

Total cost combines the regulator's own fees, company and substance costs, and our professional fees. These vary widely by jurisdiction, so we provide an itemised estimate after the discovery call.

Which licence lets me accept UK players?

Offering gambling to customers in Great Britain requires a licence from the Gambling Commission. An offshore licence does not allow you to target GB customers.

Do you work with B2B suppliers?

Yes. We support platform, game and service providers with supplier licences and registrations, including the new B2B framework in Curaçao.
